What is the procedure for high vibration alarms on Centrifugal Pump P-102A during crude transfer?
According to the Centrifugal Pump Centaur 50 SOP (Section 4.3: Mechanical Fault Triage), follow these procedural steps:
1. Cavitation Check: Inspect the suction suction line pressure gauge (PI-1021). If suction pressure is below 2.4 bar, increase supply tank head pressure immediately.
2. Bearing Temperature: Verify RTD sensors TE-102A/B. If bearing temperature exceeds 82°C (180°F), initiate a controlled trip to spare pump P-102B.
3. Seal Flush Plan: Check Plan 53A barrier fluid reserve pot level and ensure pressure is maintained 1.4 bar above stuffing box pressure.
Sources: Centaur_50_SOP.pdf — Page 42 API_610_Standards.pdf — Sec 6.3
